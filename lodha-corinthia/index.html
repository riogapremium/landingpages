About Lodha Group
Lodha Group, now operating as Macrotech Developers, is one of India’s premier real estate companies, renowned for its luxury residential and commercial developments. Established in 1980 by Mangal Prabhat Lodha, the company is headquartered in Mumbai and has expanded its footprint across India and internationally.​ Lodha Group emphasizes sustainable development and community building, aiming to create not just structures but holistic living environments. Their projects often incorporate green spaces, modern amenities, and are designed to foster community engagement.

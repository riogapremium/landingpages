About Birla Anayu
Birla Anayu at Malabar Hill Walkeshwar offers an unparalleled living experience with its exclusive 4 BHK and 5 BHK duplex. Nestled in a prime location on Walkeshwar, Malabar Hill main road, just 400 meters from the Jain Temple, this project features 7 luxurious residences spread across 14 floors. Each floor is dedicated to a single apartment Ensuring exclusivity & privacy of you and your family.

About Birla Estates
From the pre-independence era to the post-liberalisation times, there is one name that is synonymous with India’s growth as a global power – Birla. Rich in values, the Birla legacy has been passed on seamlessly from one generation to the next. From creating growth engines for the nation in the form of a global business to world-famous institutes of academic excellence and multi-speciality hospitals, the group is now dedicated to carrying the Birla legacy of excellence and building new frontiers with Birla Estates.
